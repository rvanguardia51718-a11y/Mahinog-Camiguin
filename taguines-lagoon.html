Good to know

About Taguines Lagoon

Taguines Lagoon is a tranquil freshwater lagoon nestled within the lush countryside of Mahinog. Surrounded by coconut trees, nipa huts, and open green spaces, it provides a relaxing setting for visitors looking to enjoy nature at a slower pace.

The calm, shallow waters make the lagoon suitable for light recreational activities such as paddling and sightseeing. Local families often gather here for picnics, making it a welcoming place to experience the laid-back atmosphere of rural Camiguin.

Its peaceful scenery and cool surroundings make Taguines Lagoon an ideal destination for quiet afternoons, casual walks, and outdoor relaxation.

Best For

  • Picnics
  • Family outings
  • Relaxation
  • Sightseeing
  • Nature photography
