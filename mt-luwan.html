About Mt. Luwan

Mt. Luwan is a scenic mountain located in Mahinog, offering visitors an opportunity to experience Camiguin's rich natural landscape beyond its famous beaches. Covered with lush tropical vegetation, the mountain features cool air, peaceful surroundings, and rewarding views overlooking the island and nearby coastline.

The area is popular among hikers, outdoor enthusiasts, and nature lovers seeking a quieter adventure. Depending on the trail taken, visitors can enjoy forests, rolling hills, and scenic viewpoints that showcase the island's volcanic terrain and surrounding sea.

Mt. Luwan provides a refreshing escape into nature, making it an excellent destination for those looking to experience the less-traveled side of Camiguin.
