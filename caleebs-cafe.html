About Caleeb's Cafe

Caleeb's Cafe is a cozy local café near Benoni Port, known for its relaxed atmosphere and affordable comfort food. It serves coffee, fruit shakes, milk tea, light meals, grilled specialties, and snacks — a convenient stop for both residents and travelers.

Its welcoming ambiance and friendly service have made it a popular place to enjoy a casual meal, grab a quick bite before catching the ferry, or unwind with coffee after exploring nearby attractions.
