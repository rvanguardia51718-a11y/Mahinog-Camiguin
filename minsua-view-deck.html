Good to know

About Minsua View Deck

Perched on a hill in Barangay San Jose, Minsua View Deck offers one of Mahinog's most impressive panoramic viewpoints. From the viewing area, visitors can admire sweeping views of Camiguin's rolling mountains, lush forests, nearby communities, and the sparkling waters surrounding the island.

The viewpoint is especially popular during sunrise and late afternoon when the changing light creates spectacular scenery perfect for photography. The cool breeze and peaceful surroundings make it an excellent place to relax while appreciating the island's natural beauty.

Whether stopping for a quick photo or spending time enjoying the landscape, Minsua View Deck provides a memorable perspective of eastern Camiguin.
