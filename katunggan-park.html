Katunggan Park

A peaceful boardwalk trail through century-old mangrove forest, home to native birds and quiet nature walks.

Good to know

About Katunggan Park

Katunggan Park is a community-managed mangrove eco-park located in Mahinog, Camiguin. The park features an elevated wooden boardwalk that winds through a thriving mangrove forest, allowing visitors to experience one of the island's most important coastal ecosystems up close.

Mangrove forests serve as natural barriers against coastal erosion and provide essential habitats for fish, crabs, birds, and other wildlife. Along the trail, visitors can enjoy peaceful surroundings, observe native bird species, learn about environmental conservation, and capture beautiful photographs of the lush landscape.

The calm atmosphere makes Katunggan Park an excellent destination for families, nature enthusiasts, photographers, and anyone seeking a quiet escape from busy tourist areas.
