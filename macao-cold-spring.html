Good to know

About Macao Cold Spring

Macao Cold Spring is one of Mahinog's refreshing natural attractions, famous for its crystal-clear spring water that flows directly from underground sources. The naturally cool water provides a welcome escape from the tropical heat, making it a favorite destination for both locals and tourists.

Unlike many developed resorts, Macao Cold Spring maintains much of its natural setting, surrounded by lush vegetation and peaceful countryside. Visitors can swim in the spring's clean waters, relax beneath the shade of nearby trees, or simply enjoy the calm atmosphere.

The spring's clear blue water and untouched surroundings offer an authentic nature experience that highlights the beauty of Camiguin's volcanic landscape.
