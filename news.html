Urine Dipstick Screening for Grade 1 Learners

The Rural Health Unit (RHU) of Mahinog successfully conducted a Urine Dipstick Screening for all eligible Grade 1 learners in the Municipality of Mahinog from June 23–30, 2026.

LGU Mahinog Receives New Patient Transport Vehicle

Mayor Rey Lawrence K. Tan officially received a new Patient Transport Vehicle during a turnover ceremony in Butuan City. The vehicle is expected to strengthen the municipality's emergency medical transport services and improve healthcare access for residents.

State of the Municipality Address (S.O.M.A.) 2026

The Municipality of Mahinog released highlights of the 2026 State of the Municipality Address, presenting accomplishments, ongoing infrastructure projects, public service initiatives, and future development plans under the current administration.

Mahinog Foundation Day 2026 Celebration

Residents and visitors celebrated Mahinog Foundation Day with cultural presentations, sports activities, community events, and recognition of local achievements. The celebration highlighted the municipality's heritage and community spirit.
