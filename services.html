How We Serve the Community

Municipal Services

The Municipality of Mahinog is committed to providing efficient, accessible, and people-centered public services for residents, businesses, and visitors — from healthcare and public safety to tourism, agriculture, and social welfare.

Governance & Admin Health & Welfare Economy & Environment Safety & Emergency Community Outreach

Governance & Administration

Office of the Mayor

Oversees local programs, infrastructure projects, disaster response, tourism development, and community initiatives to improve quality of life in Mahinog.

Public assistance · Community development · Infrastructure projects · Tourism promotion · Disaster response coordination

Business & Licensing

Assists entrepreneurs and investors in starting and operating businesses through the Business Permit and Licensing Office.

Business permit applications and renewals · Mayor's permit · Occupancy permit assistance · Local tax payments

Civil Registry

Manages civil records and assists residents with legal documents through the Local Civil Registry.

Birth, marriage, and death registration · Marriage license processing · Certified civil registry documents

Health & Welfare

Rural Health Unit (RHU)

Provides primary healthcare services, preventive care, maternal and child health programs, immunization, and medical consultations.

Medical consultation · Immunization · Maternal & child care · Family planning · TB services · Nutrition programs

Social Welfare & Development

The MSWDO implements programs that support vulnerable individuals, senior citizens, persons with disabilities, women, children, and families in need.

Financial & livelihood assistance · Senior citizen & PWD programs · Women's programs · Child protection · Emergency relief

Economy, Tourism & Environment

Agriculture & Fisheries

The Municipal Agriculture Office supports local farmers and fisherfolk through technical assistance and livelihood programs.

Farm technical assistance · Seed distribution · Livestock programs · Fisheries support · Coastal resource management

Tourism Office

Promotes Mahinog's natural attractions and assists visitors throughout their stay.

Tourist information · Tour assistance · Destination promotion · Tourism events · Visitor guidance

Environmental Services

Promotes environmental protection through waste management, coastal conservation, and tree-planting initiatives.

Solid waste management · Coastal clean-up · Tree planting · Environmental awareness campaigns

Safety & Emergency

Disaster Risk Reduction & Management (MDRRMO)

Prepares the municipality for emergencies through disaster preparedness, rescue operations, and public safety programs.

Emergency response · Rescue operations · Disaster preparedness training · Early warning systems · Evacuation coordination

Public Safety

Provided through the Mahinog Municipal Police Station, Bureau of Fire Protection, and other emergency responders to help maintain peace and order.

Police assistance · Fire protection · Emergency response · Traffic assistance · Community safety programs

Community Outreach

People's Day Program

Brings government services directly to barangays so residents can access healthcare, agriculture, social welfare, and other frontline services closer to their communities.

Health consultations · Agricultural assistance · Social welfare services · Civil registry assistance · Public information
