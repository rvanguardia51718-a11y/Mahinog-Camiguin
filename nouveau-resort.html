About Nouveau Resort

Nouveau Resort sits in Tupsan Pequeño, offering diners a peaceful, upscale setting overlooking the sea. The menu blends international dishes with local Camiguin flavors, making it a good choice for a relaxed sit-down meal after a day of island hopping.

The restaurant's open, breezy dining area is designed to make the most of its coastal views, and it's a popular stop for visitors looking for a slightly more refined dining experience than the local carinderias nearby.

Best For

  • Sit-down dining
  • Date nights
  • Scenic views
  • International & local cuisine
