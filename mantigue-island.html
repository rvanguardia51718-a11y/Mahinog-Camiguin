Mantigue Island

A small coral islet ringed by white sand and clear turquoise water, just a short boat ride from Mahinog.

Good to know

About Mantigue Island

Mantigue Island is one of Camiguin's most beautiful offshore destinations, located about 30 minutes by boat from the port of Mahinog. Despite being only a small coral island, it is surrounded by powdery white sand, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and thriving coral reefs that make it a favorite destination for both locals and visitors.

The island is part of the Mantigue Island Nature Park and Marine Sanctuary, where colorful tropical fish, healthy corals, and diverse marine life can be seen while snorkeling or diving. Swimming, beach relaxation, picnics, and short island tours are popular activities, while the calm waters provide an excellent environment for beginners exploring the underwater world.

Although a small community lives on the island, much of Mantigue remains protected to preserve its natural beauty. Visitors can enjoy a peaceful tropical escape while appreciating the island's rich marine ecosystem.
